Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 • Version 2.0 (IS 10262:2019 Pro Engine)

1. Client-Side Deterministic Privacy

MixPro 2019 executes 100% of concrete mix proportioning equations directly in your web browser's JavaScript V8 runtime. Proprietary laboratory trial quantities, aggregate specific gravities, moisture corrections, and project mix details are never uploaded, parsed, or retained on any remote server.

2. Telemetry & Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

We utilize Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, anonymized usage telemetry solely for improving calculation performance and UI responsiveness. This includes events such as button clicks, preset triggers (e.g. Annex A vs Annex B), and device viewport dimensions. No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is captured.

3. Local Storage

Browser local storage may be utilized to persist your custom aggregate specific gravities or lab batch volume preferences across page refreshes for your convenience. You can clear this data at any time via your browser's storage settings.

4. Engineering Disclaimer

While this application strictly follows the mathematical equations and tabular lookups stipulated in IS 10262:2019 and IS 456:2000, all generated proportions must be verified through physical laboratory trial mixes (Clause 6) and cube compressive testing at 7 and 28 days before commercial batching.
